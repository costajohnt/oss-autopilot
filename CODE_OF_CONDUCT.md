# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Our Standards

**Expected behavior:**

- Use welcoming and inclusive language
- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully
- Focus on what is best for the community

**Unacceptable behavior:**

- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments
- Harassment in any form
- Publishing others' private information without permission

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (issues, PRs, discussions) and when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior by opening an issue or contacting the maintainer directly. All complaints will be reviewed and addressed.
